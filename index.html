Our Story

Linn Park Golf Course served the communities of Castlemilk, Simshill and the wider Glasgow Southside for decades. It was where generations learned the game, where young people from Castlemilk found a space to grow, and where ordinary Glaswegians could play affordable golf on their doorstep.

Glasgow City Council closed it in March 2020 during the pandemic. Five years later, there is still no reopening plan and no timescale. The fairways fell silent. The greens disappeared under years of growth. The clubhouse was barricaded.

“It’s not Augusta, but it’s ours. The amenities in Glasgow were never meant to turn a profit — they are there for the people of Glasgow because we stay here, we pay our council tax.”
Steven McKinney, Volunteer & Former Member

So three lads from Castlemilk — Steven, Eddie and Bob — picked up strimmers and started cutting. What began as selfish reasons (they just wanted somewhere to hit a golf ball) snowballed into something bigger. 15 holes. A GoFundMe. National press. And now a formal bid to take the course back as a community trust.

The council has no plan to reopen Linn Park. We do. We are forming the South Glasgow Community Golf Trust — a registered Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation (SCIO) — to formally apply for a Community Asset Transfer and bring the course back into community hands, for good.
